Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does Mod Alpha do?
Mod Alpha analyzes historical fund data to suggest statistically better SIP investment dates each month. We never place trades — you always invest yourself, on your own broker app, based on the notification we send.
Does Mod Alpha invest money on my behalf?
No. Mod Alpha never accesses your bank or demat account and never executes trades. We only analyze public fund data and send you a notification — you decide if and when to invest, on your own broker app.
How accurate are the AI-suggested dates?
Our backtested models show the AI-suggested dates have historically reduced average entry volatility by up to 23% compared to fixed-date investing. Past performance is not a guarantee of future results.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. There's no lock-in — you can cancel anytime from the Subscription page, and you'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.
Is my data and fund information secure?
We only read publicly available fund data (NAV history, volatility, categories). We never ask for or store your bank, demat, or broker login credentials.
Which brokers does Mod Alpha work with?
Mod Alpha works alongside any broker or mutual fund platform you already use — we send you a notification, and you complete the investment on your own app.
